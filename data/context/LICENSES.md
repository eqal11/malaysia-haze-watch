# Land context data attribution and licences

The MIT licence in the repository root applies to application code, not to all data.

- **Peat polygons:** Xu, Jiren, Morris, Paul J., Liu, Junguo and Holden, Joseph (2017), *PEATMAP: Refining estimates of global peatland distribution based on a meta-analysis*, University of Leeds, [doi:10.5518/252](https://doi.org/10.5518/252). [Download record and CC BY 4.0](https://archive.researchdata.leeds.ac.uk/251/). [Licence text](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode). Derived SEA subset: repaired polygon components, clipped to 95°E–120°E / 5°S–8°N, simplified at 25 m in EPSG:6933 with topology preservation and rounded to 0.000001°. The original `AREA` attribute is not used to infer land classes.
- **Land cover:** Zanaga et al. (2022), *ESA WorldCover 10 m 2021 v200*, [doi:10.5281/zenodo.7254221](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.7254221), CC BY 4.0. **© ESA WorldCover project 2021 / Contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data (2021) processed by ESA WorldCover consortium.** This derived raster uses supplied level-64 categorical overviews and nearest-neighbour reprojection to a 0.006° grid, about 667 m at the equator. It is **not** the native 10 m product. [Official manual, licence §5.1 and class table §3.3.1](https://esa-worldcover.s3.eu-central-1.amazonaws.com/v200/2021/docs/WorldCover_PUM_V2.0.pdf).
- **Malaysia and Indonesia ADM1:** geoBoundaries gbOpen release `9469f09`, underlying OpenStreetMap / Wambacher boundaries representing 2017. © OpenStreetMap contributors. [ODbL 1.0 licence](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/), [OSM copyright](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright), [Indonesia metadata](https://www.geoboundaries.org/api/current/gbOpen/IDN/ADM1/). The Indonesia subset is clipped; Malaysia uses the existing project ADM1 file. `sources.json` preserves the inspected metadata and download URL.

The distributed attributed detection database and enriched historical detection databases are offered under **ODbL 1.0**, with the above source attributions and the CC BY 4.0 notices retained for peat/cover components. Original NASA observations retain NASA's applicable [FIRMS data policy and citation requirements](https://www.earthdata.nasa.gov/data/tools/firms). This does not apply an MIT licence to third-party data or imply endorsement.

These datasets describe mapped land context. They do not identify ignition mechanisms, operators, land ownership, responsibility or haze transport. Refer to `sources.json` for all 39 actual WorldCover tile URLs and SHA-256 checksums of the bundled analysis inputs.
